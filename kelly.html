Biography

Connecting law, communications, business, and policy.

Kelly Wilson is a lawyer, communications strategist, and policy advisor who brings a broad perspective to high-stakes legal and business challenges. Her experience spans general counsel work, corporate litigation, employment law, crisis communications, public policy, and advocacy, enabling her to align legal and communications strategies with business objectives.

Kelly is sought after for matters involving legal, reputational, and institutional risk. She has advised clients in internationally covered cases, worked with Congress, collaborated with military leadership, and translated advocacy into federal policy. She helped lead the effort addressing unsafe military housing and drafted the first version of the Military Housing Privatization Tenant Bill of Rights, key provisions of which became federal law.

As general counsel to assisted living owners and operators, Kelly advises on employment and operational matters, manages litigation, and mitigates risk. Her mediation experience strengthens her ability to reduce volatility, explain complex issues clearly, and move stakeholders toward solutions.
